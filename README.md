# 🩺 Mediqo

> **Find the right doctor, wherever you are.**

Mediqo is a doctor-first healthcare discovery platform designed to help people find relevant doctors based on their actual healthcare needs, location, language, availability, cost and other useful information.

---

## **1. 🧩 THE PROBLEM**

**Finding a hospital is easy. Finding the right doctor is not.**

When people are in an unfamiliar city, especially while travelling, finding healthcare can become stressful. Maps and directories can show nearby clinics or hospitals, but patients may still wonder:

- 🩺 Who should I consult?
- 🎯 Does the doctor treat my problem?
- 🗣️ Can they communicate in my language?
- 💰 How much will it cost?
- 🕐 Are they available?
- ⭐ Can I trust the information?

The real gap is not simply finding a healthcare location. It is making a more informed choice about which doctor may fit the patient's needs.

---

## **2. 💡 THE SOLUTION — MEDIQO**

**Mediqo is a doctor-first healthcare discovery platform that helps people find relevant doctors based on their actual needs.**

Instead of making users search through multiple listings and compare everything themselves, Mediqo brings useful information together and uses AI to understand what the patient is looking for.

For example:

> 💬 **"I'm in Lucknow, I need an English-speaking dermatologist under ₹1,000."**

Mediqo can understand these requirements, search its doctor network, and show relevant doctors with an explanation of why they match.

---

## **3. 🚀 FEATURES THAT SOLVE THE PROBLEM**

| Feature | How it helps |
|---|---|
| 🤖 **AI-Powered Doctor Matching** | Understands natural-language requests and matches patients using specialty, location, language, budget, availability and verified information. |
| 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Network** | Professional doctor profiles with qualifications, specialization, experience, languages, fees, practice locations, availability and verified credentials. |
| ⭐ **Patient Reviews** | Structured feedback on communication, professionalism, waiting experience and overall experience—not just a single rating. |
| 📍 **Location-Based Discovery** | Helps patients discover relevant doctors based on where they are or where they need care. |
| 🗣️ **Language Matching** | Helps patients find doctors who speak their preferred language, especially useful for travellers and people new to a city. |
| 💰 **Cost & Availability** | Shows consultation fees and availability where the information is provided, helping patients plan before contacting a doctor. |
| 📝 **Doctor Content & Professional Identity** | Doctors can share health-related content and professional updates, making the platform more than a simple directory. |
| 🏥 **Healthcare Access** | Provides options such as contact, appointment requests and directions so patients can move from discovery to action. |

---

## **4. 👨‍⚕️ THE DOCTOR NETWORK**

**The Doctor Network is the core of Mediqo.**

Instead of treating doctors as entries in a directory, Mediqo gives them a professional identity on the platform.

Patients can learn about:

- 🎓 Qualifications
- 🩺 Specialization
- 💼 Experience
- 🗣️ Languages
- 💰 Consultation information
- 🕐 Availability
- ✅ Verified credentials

Doctors can share educational content and professional updates, while patients can provide structured feedback.

This creates a **two-sided network** where doctors can build their professional presence and patients can make more informed healthcare choices.
